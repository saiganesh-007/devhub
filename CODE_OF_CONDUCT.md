# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

DevHub is intended to be a welcoming open-source project where people can collaborate constructively regardless of experience level or background.

## Expected behavior

Contributors and maintainers are expected to:

- communicate respectfully and constructively
- give technical feedback about the work rather than the person
- welcome reasonable questions from new contributors
- respect differing viewpoints and experiences
- avoid harassment, threats, discrimination, or deliberate disruption
- keep project spaces focused on productive collaboration

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, unwanted sexual attention, doxxing, threats, sustained trolling, or other behavior that makes participation unsafe is not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, remove, reject, or lock contributions or conversations that violate these expectations. Repeated or serious violations may result in temporary or permanent exclusion from project spaces.

## Reporting

If a conduct issue should not be discussed publicly, contact the maintainer privately through the contact information available on the maintainer's GitHub profile.

Project maintainers will review reports in good faith and take proportionate action where appropriate.
